## Description

Armada runs a fleet of AI coding agents against your repositories: you describe the work, Armada plans it, assigns
it to captains, reviews the result, and lands the change. This app connects to the Armada Admiral you run on your
own machine or server and puts that fleet in your pocket.

- See every voyage and mission as it runs, with its pipeline stages, logs, diffs, and test results.
- Approve or deny work that needs you, from the app or straight from a notification.
- Follow mixed pipelines that combine open models such as Qwen with frontier models such as Claude Opus and GPT,
  including every attempt, repair, and escalation a mission went through.
- Compare model profiles by their recorded results, and see which models suit which roles.
- Ask Armada questions about your fleet in plain language.
- Dispatch new missions and voyages, and manage fleets, vessels, captains, and personas.
- Lock the app with Face ID or your fingerprint, and save the server password securely on the device.

Armada is open source and self-hosted. The app needs an Armada Admiral (version 1.5.0 or later) that you can reach
from your phone; see armadago.ai to set one up. Your data stays on your server.
